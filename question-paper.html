General Instructions

  1. The question paper contains 180 compulsory multiple-choice questions divided equally among Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology.
  2. The total duration is 3 hours and the maximum possible score is 720 marks.
  3. Each correct answer carries +4 marks. Each incorrect answer carries −1 mark. An unattempted question carries zero marks.
  4. Each question has four alternatives—A, B, C and D—of which only one is the most appropriate answer.
  5. Use the response sheet supplied at the end of this paper for rough marking, or submit answers through the official online test page.
  6. Use g = 10 m s⁻² wherever explicitly stated. Otherwise use the data provided in the question.
  7. Tables and diagrams form an integral part of their respective questions.
  8. Do not consult books, notes or online sources during the examination.
